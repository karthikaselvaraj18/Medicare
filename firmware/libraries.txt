Arduino ESP8266 Core:
- esp8266 by ESP8266 Community (Version 3.1.2 or higher)
  Board URL: http://arduino.esp8266.com/stable/package_esp8266com_index.json

Required Arduino Libraries (Install via Arduino IDE Library Manager):
1. ArduinoJson (v6.21.x or higher) by Benoit Blanchon
2. NTPClient (v3.2.1) by Fabrice Weinberg
3. ESP8266HTTPClient (Built into ESP8266 core)
4. ESP8266WiFi (Built into ESP8266 core)
5. WiFiUdp (Built into ESP8266 core)
